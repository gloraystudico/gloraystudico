## About the studio

GLORAY STUDIO is an independent creative practice exploring the space between visual identity, editorial storytelling and interactive digital experiences.

We develop distinctive brand worlds through art direction, design systems, campaign imagery and motion-led web concepts. Our work combines a strong visual point of view with curiosity about how design moves, responds and feels.
